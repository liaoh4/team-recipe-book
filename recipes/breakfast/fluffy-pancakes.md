## Ingredients
- 1 cup flour
- 1 egg
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder

## Steps
1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder.
2. Whisk in the egg and milk.
3. Cook on a hot, lightly oiled pan. Flip when bubbles form.
